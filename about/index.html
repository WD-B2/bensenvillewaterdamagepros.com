About Bensenville Water Damage Pros | Local Dispatch

A local phone line that connects Bensenville households and businesses with licensed and insured restoration professionals.

(630) 580-6401

Bensenville Water Damage Pros is a dispatch line, not a single repair shop. When water or mold shows up in a home or business in Bensenville, Illinois, you call, describe the problem, and we send a licensed and insured restoration professional from the contractors in our network.

The line runs 24 hours a day, every day. Same-day response is offered when available, and the scope and price are explained before work starts.

What this business actually is

We answer the phone, ask the questions a crew will need answered, and route the job to a contractor who handles water damage and mold work in Bensenville and nearby towns. The network dispatches to several contractors, so the company that arrives depends on the type of job, the hour, and who is available.

That structure is why we describe ourselves plainly. We are the local line, and the licensed and insured restoration professionals are the ones who do the pumping, drying, tear-out, and cleanup at your property.

How a call works, step by step

  1. You call the number on this page at any hour and tell the dispatcher your address and what happened.
  2. The dispatcher asks whether the water source is stopped, what rooms are wet, and whether the power is on in those rooms.
  3. If there is a risk from electricity, gas, or sewage, you are told to get safe and call the utility or 911 first.
  4. A licensed and insured restoration professional is sent. Dispatch offers same-day response when it can, but no arrival time is promised.
  5. On site, the crew inspects the damage, then explains the scope and the price before any work starts.
  6. You decide whether to go ahead. Nothing begins until you agree.

What we do not do

Being clear about limits saves everyone time. We do not do waterproofing, and we do not repair plumbing unless a specific job says so. Our focus is the water and mold damage that follows a leak, a flood, or a failure.

We also do not approve insurance claims or decide what your policy covers. That decision belongs to your insurer, so read your policy and ask your insurer before you assume anything about coverage.

  • No waterproofing or foundation sealing.
  • No plumbing repair unless stated for a given job.
  • No claim approval and no coverage decisions.
  • No promise of an arrival time.

Standards you can ask any company about

Two published standards shape serious restoration work. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation. Homeowners can ask any company, including the ones we dispatch, whether its crews work to S500 and S520.

A good contractor will answer that question without hesitation and will describe how it documents moisture readings and drying progress. If the answer is vague, that tells you something.
