Water Damage Service Area in Bensenville, IL and Nearby

We dispatch restoration professionals to Bensenville and the surrounding DuPage and Cook County communities, day or night.

(630) 580-6401

Our water damage service area is centered on Bensenville, Illinois and reaches the neighboring towns of Wood Dale, Addison, Schiller Park, Franklin Park, and Stone Park. The line runs 24/7 and sends licensed and insured restoration professionals to homes and businesses in those places.

A same-day visit is offered when the schedule allows, and scope and price come first, then the work.

Bensenville, our home base

Bensenville is a village in DuPage County, with a business address at 110 W Irving Park Blvd. We cover ZIP codes 60106 and 60105 within the village and the immediate surrounding area. Calls from these ZIP codes are the core of our daily work.

Whether the problem is a burst pipe, a failed water heater, or a storm leak, the first step is the same. Call the line, describe the water, and a dispatcher takes it from there.

Two counties, two permit offices

Our area crosses a county line. Bensenville, Wood Dale, and Addison are in DuPage County, while Schiller Park, Franklin Park, and Stone Park are in Cook County. Permit and inspection offices differ between them, and each village has its own rules.

Most water damage cleanup does not need a permit, but rebuilding work may. Ask your village's building or community development office whether a permit applies to your repair. In Bensenville, inspections are scheduled through Community and Economic Development with at least 24 hours notice.

Services available across the area

Every job begins with stopping and removing the water. Read about water extraction for standing water, basement water damage cleanup for below-grade rooms, and structural drying and dehumidification for the equipment that dries wet framing.

Pipe failures have their own pages, including burst pipe water damage and water heater leak cleanup. If water has been sitting for days, see mold remediation. Businesses can read about commercial water damage restoration.

How coverage works on a call

When you call from within the area, the dispatcher confirms your address, asks about the source and the wet rooms, and sends a licensed and insured restoration professional. No arrival time is promised, but same-day service is offered where available.

If your address is just outside the towns listed above, call anyway. The network dispatches to several contractors, and one may be able to reach you. Tell the dispatcher the nearest cross streets, since that helps confirm the route and which contractor is closest at that hour.

Questions we hear

Which ZIP codes do you serve?

We serve ZIP codes 60106 and 60105 in Bensenville, plus 60191, 60101, 60176, 60131, and 60165 in the nearby towns.

Do you serve both DuPage and Cook County?

Yes. Bensenville, Wood Dale, and Addison are in DuPage County, and Schiller Park, Franklin Park, and Stone Park are in Cook County.

What if my town is not listed?

Call and give your address. Elmhurst, Elk Grove Village, Itasca, and Villa Park residents often call, and availability depends on the hour.

Is service available at night?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7, and dispatch aims for a same-day visit when it is available.
