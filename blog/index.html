Water Damage and Mold Guides for Bensenville, IL Homes

Guides that answer the questions people ask before and after a leak, from insurance and cost to mold and water categories.

(630) 580-6401

These guides are for the questions that come up around a water loss but are not about the cleanup itself. Does insurance cover this, what decides the price, how fast does mold start, and how dirty is this water.

Each guide is written for Bensenville homes and uses sources such as the Illinois Department of Insurance and the Illinois Department of Public Health. If water is active right now, skip the reading and call the 24/7 line.

Insurance, cost and timelines

The insurance guides explain, in general terms, which kinds of water damage policies often cover and which they often do not. They also cover what to photograph, what receipts to keep and when to report.

The cost guides list the factors that move a restoration bill, such as area, materials, equipment days and the water category. They do not quote prices, because every job is different, and the scope and price come first, then the work.

Mold and water categories

The mold guides cover what mold needs to grow, when testing makes sense and how remediation follows the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard. Health questions belong with a physician, and the guides say so.

The water category guides explain clean, gray and black water in plain terms, so you can tell whether something needs water extraction and drying or a full sewage backup cleanup. For active mold, see mold remediation.
