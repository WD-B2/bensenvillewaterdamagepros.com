Street flooding and sewer backups in Schiller Park

The Cook County hazard mitigation plan annex for Schiller Park lists these flood-prone spots: River Road between Addison Road and Foster Avenue, the Irving Park Road and Lawrence Avenue viaducts, Irving Park Road from 25th Avenue to I-294, 25th and Ivanhoe Avenues, and River Road at Susy Lane. It also records viaduct flooding on August 7, 2018, that stalled vehicles. If your route home or your delivery route uses one of those, plan an alternate before storms.

The same annex says the Des Plaines River runs along the village's east side and Crystal Creek crosses it on the way to the river. FEMA has identified 19 repetitive loss properties in the village, all single family homes. For you, the lesson is practical. Check the FEMA flood map for your address, and ask your insurer about flood coverage separately from your homeowners policy.

When water is coming into your Schiller Park building

Storm water and sewage are not the same emergency as a clean pipe leak, and the first minutes differ. Use these four steps and then call (630) 580-6401, day or night.

Find where it enters

Check whether the water comes from a pipe, a floor drain, a window well or the foundation. A pipe has a shut-off valve. Drain or floor backup means do not run sinks, showers or washers.

Power and gas first

If water is near outlets, the panel, the furnace or the water heater, stay out of the room. Call 911, or the electric or gas utility, before you call anyone about cleanup.

Save what is dry

Carry out documents, medications and small electronics from the dry side only. Stack boxes on tables, and photograph the waterline and the damaged items before moving them.

Treat it as dirty

Water that came from a sewer or from the street can carry contamination. Keep children, pets and anyone with open cuts away, and wear boots and gloves if you must cross it.

What the village and the Army Corps built on Soreng Avenue

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Chicago District, and the village broke ground July 24, 2023, on a stormwater detention pond holding about 1.5 million gallons. It sits north of Soreng Avenue and east of Emerson Drive, with a new storm sewer along Soreng Avenue that ties into the Seymour Avenue sewer at 25th Avenue. The project aims to relieve two areas that flood as street water when runoff exceeds storm sewer capacity.

A project like this reduces street flooding in the target areas, but it does not stop a sewer line backing up through a floor drain or a pipe bursting in a wall. Those are building-side problems, and they still need fast water removal.

Who to call for a backup or a water main in Schiller Park

For a sanitary sewer backup, the village asks you to call Public Works at 847-671-8550, Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. After hours, call the police non-emergency line at 847-678-4794. Public Works checks village-maintained lines first, and if those are clear, the owner hires a plumber for the service line.

Village code section 52.34(E) makes owners responsible for the building service sewer and the house connection. The village also names sanitary check valves and overhead sewer systems as ways to prevent backups during storms. When a backup has already reached the floor, our sewage backup cleanup crews extract the water and disinfect the area.

Owners and renters in a landlocked village

The Census puts Schiller Park at 59.2 percent owner-occupied, so many households rent. The hazard plan calls the village largely built out and landlocked by O'Hare, the forest preserve, Franklin Park, Rosemont and Chicago, with future development mostly redevelopment. With so many renters, landlords and tenants should agree early on who calls and who documents.

Photos with dates, receipts for emergency repairs and a written scope all help. Ask your insurer what your policy requires, and do not start permanent repairs without checking.

Drying, permits and the next steps

After extraction, crews map moisture with meters and a thermal imaging camera, set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, and log readings daily. You can ask any company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the water damage restoration standard. Ask the village Community Development office whether a permit applies before repair work.

Village Hall is at 9526 W. Irving Park Road. Mold can start where moisture lingers, and S520 is the mold remediation standard. Questions about health belong with a physician.

Did the Soreng Avenue pond stop flooding in Schiller Park?

The pond and storm sewer were built to relieve two areas that flood as street water in heavy rain. They do not stop a sewer backup or a pipe break inside a building, so keep your own protection in place.

Who do I call first for a sewer backup in Schiller Park?

Call village Public Works at 847-671-8550 on weekdays, or the police non-emergency line at 847-678-4794 after hours. Then call us for water extraction and disinfection.

Is flood damage covered by homeowners insurance?
